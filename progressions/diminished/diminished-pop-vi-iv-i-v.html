About the vi–IV–I–V

History

Starting the axis loop on vi gives it a minor feel. Boston Globe critic Marc Hirsh, who noticed it in songs of the late 1990s, called it the "sensitive female chord progression" in a 2008 article.

Listen for

The minor start: vi sets a wistful mood, and the major chords that follow lift it without ever fully resolving.

Famous songs

  • Numb Linkin Park
  • Poker Face Lady Gaga
  • Despacito Luis Fonsi feat. Daddy Yankee
