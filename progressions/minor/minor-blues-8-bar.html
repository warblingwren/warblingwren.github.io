About the 8-bar blues

History

Often called the second most common blues form, the 8-bar blues was on record by the 1920s, in songs such as "Trouble in Mind" (1926) and "How Long Blues" (1928). Big Bill Broonzy's "Key to the Highway" (1941) set the version used here, with V7 in bar 2.

Listen for

How fast it moves: V7 in bar 2, IV7 by bar 3, and a turnaround in the last two bars that sends the song straight back to the top.
