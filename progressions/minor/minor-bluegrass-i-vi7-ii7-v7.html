About the I–VI7–II7–V7

History

Known as the ragtime progression for its popularity in ragtime, this chain of dominants is much older: Liszt used it in Liebesträume (1850). It came into bluegrass through "Salty Dog Blues", whose words were printed by 1911 and which Papa Charlie Jackson recorded in 1924.

Listen for

Every chord after I is a dominant 7th, each one a fifth above the next: VI7 pulls to II7, II7 to V7, V7 to home.

Famous songs

  • Salty Dog Blues Papa Charlie Jackson; later Rev. Gary Davis
  • Alice's Restaurant Arlo Guthrie
  • Sweet Georgia Brown Ben Bernie and Maceo Pinkard
