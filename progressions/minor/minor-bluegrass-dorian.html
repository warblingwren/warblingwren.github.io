About the i–VII–i

History

"Shady Grove" is believed to come from eastern Kentucky around 1900. Its melody was published in 1915, and it moved from old-time music into the bluegrass repertoire. The tune is Dorian, a minor scale with a raised 6th; players accompany it with the minor home chord and the major chord a whole step below.

Listen for

Two chords, back and forth: the minor home chord and the major VII, a modal sound that never needs a V chord to feel home.
