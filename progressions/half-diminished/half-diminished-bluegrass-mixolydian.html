About the I–♭VII–I

History

"Old Joe Clark" was printed as sung in Virginia in 1918, and Cowan Powers and his Family Band recorded it in 1924. Its melody is Mixolydian, the major scale with a lowered 7th, which is where the ♭VII chord comes from.

Listen for

The drop to ♭VII and straight back: a rugged, old-time sound with no leading tone, so home is reached by stepping, not by resolving.
