About the Andalusian cadence (i–VII–VI–V)

History

The Andalusian cadence has deep roots in the music of southern Spain, especially flamenco. From there it spread into Latin music, then pop, rock and jazz, with hits such as "Hit the Road Jack" and "Runaway" in 1961.

Listen for

The bass walking down four steps, from i to the major V, which lands with a dramatic, Spanish-sounding pause.

Famous songs

  • Hit the Road Jack Ray Charles
  • Runaway Del Shannon
  • Stray Cat Strut Stray Cats
